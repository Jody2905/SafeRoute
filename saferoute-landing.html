How SafeRoute Works

Four simple steps from booking to safe arrival — with privacy controls at every stage.

01
📍

Book Your Ride

Enter your pickup and destination. The system securely assigns an available driver and notifies them instantly.

02
🔗

Share Securely

Generate a private time-limited tracking link. Share it with a trusted contact so they can monitor your journey.

03
🚗

Live GPS Tracking

Only you, your driver, and your trusted contacts see your real-time GPS. Others see direction only — never exact coordinates.

04
✅

Safe Arrival

When you arrive, tracking automatically stops. Trusted contacts receive a "safely arrived" notification. No lingering data.

Features

Built Around Privacy & Safety

Every feature is designed to put you in control of your own data.

🛡

Role-Based GPS Visibility

Not everyone sees the same thing. Access to live location data is controlled by role, consent, and ride status — never open by default.

🧑
Passenger Full GPS
🚗
Driver Full GPS
💙
Trusted Contact View Only
👁
Public Viewer Direction Only
🔗

Private Tracking Links

Share time-limited, token-based tracking URLs with specific people. Links expire automatically when the ride ends.

🚌

Group Ride Pickup

Enable Open Route mode so others can request pickup along your route — without ever seeing your exact GPS position.

🔔

Arrival Notifications

Trusted contacts are automatically notified the moment you safely reach your destination. No manual updates needed.

🛑

Auto Tracking Termination

GPS tracking stops the instant your ride completes. No residual tracking. No stale data. Full privacy restored.

System Users

Everyone Has Their Role

SafeRoute is designed around a clear role-based access system with distinct privileges for each user type.

🧑‍💼

Primary Passenger

Books the taxi, controls sharing settings, and holds full GPS access throughout the journey.

🚕

Taxi Driver

Sees passenger pickup and drop-off locations. Manages ride status from start to completion.

💙

Trusted Contact

Receives a private link to monitor the trip. Gets notified when the passenger safely arrives.

🙋

Pickup Passenger

Requests a ride along the route. Gains GPS access only after confirming they've been picked up.

👁

Public Viewer

Can see if a taxi is heading their direction. Never sees exact coordinates or passenger identity.

Security & Privacy

Security at Every Layer

SafeRoute is built with a cybersecurity-aware design — because location data is sensitive data.

🔐

HTTPS / TLS Encryption

All GPS data is transmitted over encrypted connections. No location data travels in plaintext.

🪙

JWT Authentication

Token-based authentication ensures only verified users access the system and their assigned data.

🛡

Role-Based Access Control

RBAC enforces that each user only accesses data appropriate to their role and active ride status.

⏱

Time-Limited Tokens

Shared tracking links expire when the ride ends. No indefinite access to anyone's location.

🗑

Data Retention Policy

GPS logs are purged after a defined retention window. You ride — we don't hoard your history.

✋

Consent-Driven Sharing

No location is ever shared without the passenger's explicit action. Full opt-in, always.
